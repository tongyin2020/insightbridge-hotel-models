### About InsightBridge Global

InsightBridge Global is a US-registered hospitality technology company (Wyoming LLC) that has developed three proprietary AI models purpose-built for the Macau SAR hotel market. Our platform, validated through a 21-day automated simulation across 425 Macau hotel properties (145 mid-market + 280 luxury segment), delivers measurable RevPAR improvement through machine learning-driven dynamic pricing, CRM integration, and autonomous direct-booking optimization.

We are aware that Shiji Daylight PMS has been selected by MGM Grand Paradise and Melco Resorts — properties that represent the exact target segment for our 4–5 star AI model. This alignment makes Shiji the ideal PMS integration partner for our go-to-market strategy in Macau.

### Our Three AI Models

**① MARE — Market-Adaptive Revenue Engine (2–3 Star Hotels)**
Processes real-time competitive market signals, occupancy forecasts, local event density, weather patterns, and ferry/border flow indicators to generate daily optimized pricing recommendations. Validated at 4.6–5% RevPAR uplift in Macau's highly competitive mid-market segment.

**② DirectorAI CRM — Integrated CRM/RMS/PMS Engine (2–3 Star Hotels)**
Bridges CRM loyalty data, booking velocity signals, and dynamic pricing logic into a unified revenue decision engine. Designed to reduce OTA commission dependency while building direct booking equity for independent and chain-managed properties.

**③ SelfACQ — Autonomous Guest Acquisition Engine (4–5 Star Hotels)**
Generates multi-channel pricing strategies with OTA/direct/VIP tiered differentials, automated bundle package generation (6+ offer types per pricing cycle), and forward-looking demand capture. Specifically calibrated for Macau's integrated resort environment, including gaming-adjacent occupancy dynamics.

### Proposed Integration Architecture

We propose a **bi-directional, real-time API integration** between InsightBridge and Shiji Daylight PMS:

**Inbound data flow (PMS → InsightBridge AI Engine):**
- Current occupancy rate and remaining inventory
- Booking velocity (24h pick-up pace)
- Historical ADR and RevPAR by room category
- Cancellation rate signals
- Guest segment distribution (via CRM linkage)

**Outbound data flow (InsightBridge → PMS):**
- Daily AI-optimized rate recommendations per room category
- 7-day forward pricing curve
- Channel-differentiated pricing (OTA / Direct / Corporate / VIP)
- Automated rate code updates (with configurable override controls for Revenue Managers)

This architecture mirrors Shiji's existing integration model with IDeaS, Duetto, and FLYR — we are proposing to join this certified RMS partner ecosystem as a specialized AI layer optimized for the Macau/Greater China market.

**Market Differentiation:** As Shiji expands its Daylight PMS presence in Macau and Greater China, InsightBridge provides an AI revenue optimization capability calibrated specifically for the region's unique demand dynamics — a gap not fully addressed by European/US-centric RMS providers.

**Ecosystem Stickiness:** Hotels using both Shiji PMS and InsightBridge AI become deeply integrated customers of both platforms, increasing retention and platform dependency for Shiji.

**Data Flywheel:** Our models improve with more real-world data. Greater Macau PMS integration coverage accelerates model accuracy, creating a compounding advantage that benefits all Shiji-connected properties in the region.

**Immediate Pilot Opportunity:** We are actively seeking 1–2 Macau 4-star properties for a Shadow Mode validation pilot (read-only PMS data access, zero operational disruption). A Shiji-connected property would be ideal for this first real-world validation phase.
